# Absorb

This is a Helpful Condition.

Absorb provides temporary Hit Points that are lost first when the Squaddie takes damage. They cannot be healed but they
can exceed the Squaddie's maximum Hit Points.

# Examples

These assume the Squaddie has a maximum of 5 Hit Points and 1 Absorb.

## Temporary Hit Points are lost first

If the Squaddie takes 1 damage, they lose the 1 Absorb and are left with 5/5 Hit Points.

### Cannot heal Absorb hit points

Healing doesn't restore the temporary hit points. The squaddie is at 5/5 Hit Points and would not benefit.

## Survives 5 damage

If the Squaddie is hit for 5 damage, they lost the 1 Absorb first and are left with 1/5 Hit Points.
